Privacy Policy — Maker & Seller Formulas

Last updated: 2026-11-05 (America/New_York).

Maker & Seller Formulas (“the Add-on”) is a Google Sheets add-on provided by Anthony Crawford / Kitcroft (“we”, “us”).

What the Add-on does

The Add-on runs formulas and a structure-drawing sidebar inside your Google Sheet. Chemistry, fee, and shop calculations run in your spreadsheet. Drawn structures are processed in the Add-on to show formula and molecular weight; that processing happens in Google Apps Script for your session.

Data we access

With your permission (Google OAuth), the Add-on may:

  • Read and write the Google Sheet you have open (spreadsheets.currentonly) so custom functions and “Insert into cells” can work.
  • Show menus and sidebars (script.container.ui).
  • Call Stripe’s API over HTTPS (script.external_request) when you use Activate Pro, to verify that the email you enter matches a paid checkout for this product.

We do not request access to all of your Drive files, Gmail, or Contacts.

License / payment data

If you buy Pro, payment is processed by Stripe. At activation you enter the email address you used at checkout; the Add-on sends that email to Stripe solely to look up your paid Checkout Session / subscription and confirm it is not refunded, canceled, or (for a one-month pass) past its 30-day window. We store in Google Apps Script Document Properties for the open sheet: that email, plan, and Pro cache expiry. We do not cap how many sheets you activate. We do not receive or store full card numbers. We do not email a separate license key.

Third-party services

  • Google — hosts Sheets and Apps Script; see Google Privacy Policy.
  • Stripe — payment processing and purchase verification; see Stripe Privacy Policy.
  • jsDelivr / JSME — the Draw structure sidebar loads the JSME editor from a public CDN in your browser. JSME is open source under the BSD-3-Clause license.

How long we keep data

Pro cache on a sheet expires automatically (within 12 hours, or sooner when a one-month pass ends). You can clear it via the Add-on menu. You can ask us to delete support records by emailing support (subject to legal retention needs).

Children

The Add-on is intended for general audiences including students. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 except as needed to deliver a license a parent or school purchased. Contact support to delete such data.
